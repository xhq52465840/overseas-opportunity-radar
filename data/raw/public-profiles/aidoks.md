## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A complete toolkit to create, edit, validate, view and convert e-invoices — from PDF to XML and back.
- Problem solved: Converts paper or PDF invoices into structured PEPPOL BIS 3.0 e-invoices without prior knowledge of UBL XML, EN 16931 or e-invoicing standards.
- Pricing model: Free: AI OCR for PDF-to-e-invoice conversion works on one PDF a month without an account; a free account raises that to 3.
- Target persona: Businesses needing e-invoicing solutions in the EU for compliance with PEPPOL BIS 3.0 and EN 16931 standards.
