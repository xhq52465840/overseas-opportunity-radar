## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A super-viral timer game you see on social media: hide the clock, hit the buzzer, and find out who has the best internal clock. Works solo or passing one phone around a group, and the blind reveal films well for short-form video.
- Problem solved: Groups want a quick, zero-setup game to play on one phone at parties, bars and family gatherings, with no accounts, no Wi-Fi and no learning curve. Solo players get a daily skill challenge that takes under a minute.
- Pricing model: Free Version + Paid $3.99 to unlock different game modes.
- Target persona: Casual gamers, friend groups and families
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)

- Estimated user count: 30,000
