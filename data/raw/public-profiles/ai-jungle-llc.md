## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Create personalized, AI-made short films featuring loved ones or customers, and send them as private links or branded thank-you gifts.
- Problem solved: Makes it quick and memorable to celebrate or thank someone, while helping businesses turn customer appreciation into word-of-mouth referrals.
- Pricing model: First 2 videos free; then pay per video using banana credits. Packs range from $4.99 for 5 bananas to $4,990 for 20,000 bananas; one banana costs at most $1 and less in larger packs.
- Target persona: People creating personalized gifts for friends and family, and businesses thanking customers, colleagues, or employees; agencies and creators are also prospective audiences.
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
