## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Synq helps Shopify trading card game shops add inventory, automatically reprice cards to market, and manage online and in-store sales from one place.
- Problem solved: Card shops spend time manually listing new stock, tracking market prices, and keeping inventory and prices consistent across online and in-store sales.
- Pricing model: Free for up to 100 cards; Starter $29/month for up to 2,000 cards; Pro $79/month for up to 15,000 cards; Scale $149/month for up to 50,000 cards. Paid plans include a 7-day free trial; yearly billing saves 33%.
- Target persona: Shopify stores selling trading card games and collectibles, including singles and sealed products.
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Shopify app; supports TCGplayer market pricing, inventory imports, and Shopify POS. The site reports 66,000+ cards listed and 2.2 million prices checked.
- Markets: [E-comm](https://trustmrr.com/category/ecommerce), [Utilities](https://trustmrr.com/category/utilities), [Games](https://trustmrr.com/category/games), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity)
