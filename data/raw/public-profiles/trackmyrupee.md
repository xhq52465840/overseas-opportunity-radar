## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A privacy-first personal finance platform for tracking net worth, spending, investments, loans, and cash flow in one place without bank logins or SMS access.
- Problem solved: Indian salaried professionals struggle to see where their money goes and understand their overall net worth across scattered accounts, investments, and debts; many existing finance apps require invasive bank or SMS access.
- Pricing model: Free: ₹0/month; Plus: ₹499/year or ₹49/month; Pro: ₹999/year or ₹99/month. Pro includes a 7-day free trial.
- Target persona: Indian salaried professionals seeking private, unified personal finance and net worth tracking.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 317
- Additional info: Open source; founder-led by Omkar Pathak. Privacy-focused, with no bank connections or SMS reading; supports family sharing, CSV export, and English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- Markets: [Analytics](https://trustmrr.com/category/analytics), [Fintech](https://trustmrr.com/category/fintech), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas)
