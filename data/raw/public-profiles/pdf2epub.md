## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Converts PDFs into reader-ready EPUBs with properly reconstructed paragraphs and chapters, plus OCR for scanned pages.
- Problem solved: PDFs often preserve printed line breaks and lack usable chapter structure, making books difficult to read in e-readers; scanned PDFs also need OCR.
- Pricing model: Free: $0 for one PDF at a time, up to 500 pages and 200MB, with OCR for the first 20 pages. One book: $5 once for up to 2,000 pages with scans included. Shelf: $10/month for 1,000 pages monthly and files up to 2,000 pages, with batch conversion by email.
- Target persona: People who want to read PDF books on Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books, or other e-readers, including readers with scanned books.
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 170
- Additional info: Files are converted in memory and deleted after download; no signup or account is required. Supports EPUB3 and includes chapter-level footnotes. Paid conversions have a 30-day refund guarantee.
- Markets: [Utilities](https://trustmrr.com/category/utilities), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity)
