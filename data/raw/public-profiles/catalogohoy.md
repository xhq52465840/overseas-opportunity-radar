## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: All-in-one platform for small businesses in Latin America to sell online: product catalog, orders, POS, customers, expenses and sales metrics, from the web or the iOS/Android app.
- Problem solved: Small businesses in Latin America sell through WhatsApp and Instagram with messy spreadsheets. CatalogoHoy gives them a shareable online catalog, order and stock tracking, and AI tools to load products in minutes.
- Pricing model: Monthly or annual (50% off): Basic $11.99/mo, Pro $19.99/mo, Advanced $34.99/mo, plus custom Enterprise deals. Extra AI credits are sold as add-ons.
- Target persona: Small and medium retailers in Latin America (mainly Venezuela): clothing, cosmet
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 5,000
- Additional info: AI WhatsApp assistant to manage the store by chat, AI product import from invoices, PDFs and spreadsheets, background removal and image generation. Public API + MCP, team roles, POS with barcode scanner, automated email/WhatsApp notifications. [Private metric reference available after login.]
