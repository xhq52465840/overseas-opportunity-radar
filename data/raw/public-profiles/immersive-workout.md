## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Turns a 3-minute workout into an arcade game where your body is the controller, using only the camera you already own
- Problem solved: Home workouts are boring and easy to skip, and fitness games usually need a console, VR headset or wearables
- Pricing model: 3-day free trial, then $4.99/month or $39.99/year
- Target persona: Busy adults and families who want to move more at home without equipment
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, laptops and TVs (via HDMI). Pose tracking runs on-device, so camera video is never uploaded.
- Markets: [Health & Fitness](https://trustmrr.com/category/health-fitness), [Games](https://trustmrr.com/category/games)
