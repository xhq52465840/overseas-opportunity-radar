## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: 10015.io is a free, all-in-one toolbox of fast, well-made online tools for text, images, PDFs, code, colors and social media, with no sign-up, no install and no watermarks, and most files never leave your browser.
- Problem solved: 10015.io saves people from hunting through ad-heavy, sign-up-walled single-purpose sites for everyday tasks like converting a PDF, formatting code, editing an image or making a social media mockup, and from uploading private files to unknown servers to do it.
- Pricing model: All tools are free with no sign-up or limits; makers can optionally pay to feature their product in Product Finder.
- Target persona: Developers, Social Media Consultants, Educators and Students
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 80,000
- Additional info: Browser extensions for Chrome (10,000+ users, rated 4.7/5) and Firefox (rated 4.7/5). About 150,000 visits a month from Google search. Most tools run entirely in your browser, so files are never uploaded. Free, with no sign-up.
- Markets: [Dev Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/developer-tools), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity), [Marketing](https://trustmrr.com/category/marketing), [Design Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/design-tools), [Utilities](https://trustmrr.com/category/utilities)
