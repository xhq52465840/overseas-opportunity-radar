## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: AI video editing that lets users change objects, people, clothing, products, backgrounds, and styles in footage they have already shot using plain-language requests.
- Problem solved: Making visual changes to existing video footage is difficult and time-consuming with traditional editing tools; Aisma lets users alter what appears in a clip while preserving its motion, framing, length, and original audio.
- Pricing model: Paid with credits: 10 credits per second at 720p or 20 credits per second at 1080p. Credits are sold in one-time packs or weekly plans; prices are shown before generation.
- Target persona: Creators, small businesses, and marketers who want to edit existing video footage, including realtors, fitness creators, clothing shops, and ecommerce sellers.
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Available on the web, iPhone, and Android. Supports MP4 and MOV clips of 2–60 seconds, with up to 10 reference photos per edit.
- Markets: [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [Content Creation](https://trustmrr.com/category/content-creation), [Design Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/design-tools), [Mobile Apps](https://trustmrr.com/category/mobile-apps)
