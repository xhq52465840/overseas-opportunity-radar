## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Turns PDF bank and credit card statements into clean transactions in Excel, CSV, OFX, QFX, QBO or Google Sheets, checked against the balances printed on the statement.
- Problem solved: Banks often give only PDF statements, or limit CSV history. Without BankConv, accountants and small businesses retype transactions or fix broken copy-paste before importing into QuickBooks, Xero or a spreadsheet.
- Pricing model: $2.99/statement (10 pages, then $0.20/page). Plans: $29/mo (500 pages), $49/mo (1,000), $79/mo (5,000); yearly $228/$348/$588.
- Target persona: Accountants, bookkeepers, small businesses and freelancers
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 1 person
- Estimated user count: 1,288
- Additional info: 48,900 pages converted across 9,300 statements since launch. Google Sheets add-on on the Workspace Marketplace, REST API and remote MCP server. App and site in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. Bing Copilot cites the site about 100 times a day.
- Markets: [Fintech](https://trustmrr.com/category/fintech), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity)
