## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A first edit of your talking video in minutes: ums and retakes cut, captions in your style, music and camera moves, and it's still yours to edit.
- Problem solved: Editing a talking video takes far longer than recording it. Yappilot does the slow part (cuts, captions, music, framing), so creators post more and editors finish more videos.
- Pricing model: Free: 1 hour of footage a month, no card. Creator: $19/month (₹799), 10 hours. Pro: $39/month (₹1,599), 30 hours.
- Target persona: Creators, coaches, podcasters and streamers, video editors
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Edits run in the browser, so your video never leaves your computer. Captions in 118 languages, Hinglish in Roman or Devanagari. Exports to Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve and After Effects. Works with Claude and ChatGPT agents (MCP).
- Markets: [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [Content Creation](https://trustmrr.com/category/content-creation), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas), [Productivity](https://trustmrr.com/category/productivity)
