## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Turns one topic into a finished, published faceless YouTube video, fully automated
- Problem solved: Running a faceless channel means juggling separate tools for scripts, voice, visuals and editing, and hours of glue work per video
- Pricing model: Free: 200 credits, no card. Starter $19/mo, Creator $49/mo, Studio $99/mo, Scale $249/mo. Credit top-ups from $10
- Target persona: Faceless YouTube creators, niche channel operators, and marketers who want video
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Direct YouTube publishing via the YouTube Data API. Live cost estimate before every render, and failed renders are never charged. Shorts (9:16) and long-form (16:9) from the same pipeline. Launched on Product Hunt and Fazier.
- Markets: [Social Media](https://trustmrr.com/category/social-media), [Content Creation](https://trustmrr.com/category/content-creation)
