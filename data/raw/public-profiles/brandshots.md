## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Creates studio-quality product photos, social posts, and ad creatives from a single product photo, with brand styling applied automatically.
- Problem solved: Online sellers need professional, on-brand product imagery and ad content but face the expense and time of hiring photographers or designers and producing creative assets for multiple channels.
- Pricing model: Free: $0 with 5 signup credits. Starter: $119/year with 100 credits/month. Growth: $499/year with 500 credits/month. Credit-based generation; monthly plans are also available.
- Target persona: Ecommerce sellers, DTC and jewelry brands, performance marketers, creative agencies, solo founders, and social media managers.
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
