## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Get an alert the minute a sold-out gift is back on a Target shelf near you, at the store price.
- Problem solved: Hot gifts like the PS5 Pro and Switch 2 sell out in minutes, and Target doesn't announce store restocks. Shoppers refresh all day or pay scalper prices.
- Pricing model: One-time passes: $19 for 7 days, $29 Holiday Pass (until Jan 15), $49 Family Pass. Optional $9 Priority checks every 30 seconds.
- Target persona: US parents and gift shoppers hunting sold-out consoles, controllers and trading
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Free live stock check by ZIP, no signup. Public log of every Target restock we catch, with the store and how long it lasted. Live Target stock pages for 25 US cities.
- Markets: [E-comm](https://trustmrr.com/category/ecommerce), [Utilities](https://trustmrr.com/category/utilities), [Games](https://trustmrr.com/category/games), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas)
