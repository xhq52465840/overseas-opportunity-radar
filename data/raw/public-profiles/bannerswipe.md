- Value proposition: Turns one ad creative into every ad size in bulk, for media buyers and their AI agents.
- Problem solved: Resizing the same ad into dozens of network sizes by hand, with stretched, letterboxed or badly cropped results.
- Pricing model: Plans from $19/month. Previews don't use credits.
- Target persona: Marketers, advertisers, and creative teams producing recurring digital ad campaigns
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
