## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: An always-on AI teammate in Slack that answers company questions instantly, runs new hire onboarding automatically, and reviews employment contracts and policies, all for one flat monthly fee instead of per-seat pricing.
- Problem solved: Company knowledge is scattered across docs, Slack threads and people's heads. New hires and employees keep asking the same questions, senior staff lose hours answering them, onboarding is inconsistent, and HR teams send out contracts and policies without catching risky clauses or gaps.
- Pricing model: Starter: $299/mo, Growth: $499/m and Custom: quote-based
- Target persona: Heads of People, HR managers and founders at Slack-using companies with 20 to 20
