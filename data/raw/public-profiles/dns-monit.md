## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: DNS monitoring for every domain you look after: Records, email security, DNSSEC and expiry dates checked every 15 minutes with an alert the moment anything changes.
- Problem solved: Prevent costly downtime, protect email deliverability, and stay ahead of security threats by detecting DNS misconfigurations, unauthorized changes, and potential vulnerabilities.
- Pricing model: Free plan is 3 domains, checked hourly — 15-minute checks start at $9/month Domain caps per plan: 25 (Pro), 150 (Agency), 750 (MSP) and more
- Target persona: Domain administrators, Managed service providers, Agencies
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Estimated user count: 20
- Additional info: Add a domain and see every record, its health score and its grade within the hour Import a client portfolio by pasting a list or uploading a CSV with a label column Get an alert carrying the old and the new value the moment a DNS record changes Filter by what is missing — no DMARC, no DNSSEC...
- Markets: [Security](https://trustmrr.com/category/security), [Dev Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/developer-tools), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas)
