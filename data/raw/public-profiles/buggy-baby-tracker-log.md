## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A private, easy-to-use app for tracking a baby’s feeding, sleep, diapers, growth, and daily care, with logs stored on the user’s phone.
- Problem solved: Helps parents keep an organized record of baby care and growth without relying on cloud storage or creating an account.
- Pricing model: Free core tracking; optional Buggy Plus subscriptions: $1.99/week or $9.99/year, plus a $9.99 lifetime purchase. Annual plan includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.
- Target persona: Parents and caregivers of babies who want to track daily care and growth privately.
