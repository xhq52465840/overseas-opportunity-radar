## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: An online CV builder for Dutch job applications that lets users create, preview, and download a professional, ATS-friendly PDF CV for a one-time fee.
- Problem solved: Helps job seekers create a clear, polished CV without struggling with formatting or subscription-based CV services.
- Pricing model: Free to create and preview; €7.95 one-time per CV PDF download. Optional AI profile photo costs €9.99 if used.
- Target persona: Job seekers, students, and expats applying for jobs in the Netherlands.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Offers 13+ ATS-friendly templates, CV upload and autofill, and later free editing and re-downloading of a purchased CV. Hosted on EU servers in Germany.
