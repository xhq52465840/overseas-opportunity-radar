## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: ToStatic turns visual-builder sites into portable static files. Then the web app takes it from there: previews, backups, deploys, custom domains, and SEO and performance checks.
- Problem solved: ToStatic solves the problem of platform lock-in and high hosting costs by allowing users to export their visual-builder sites to static files that can be hosted anywhere.
- Pricing model: Always Free: €0/mo, Topup Voucher: €14,99, Pro User: €199,99/year, Agency: €699,99/year
- Target persona: Individuals and creators who publish regularly, agencies and power users managing many client sites
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Estimated user count: 3,500
