## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Classic 1995 minesweeper turned into a live, fair multiplayer game: the same board for everyone, server-timed runs, and rank earned only by clean play.
- Problem solved: Classic minesweeper is a solo game with nobody to beat. Minesweeper Royale adds live rivals, a daily world ranking and a rank that can't be bought.
- Pricing model: Free to play. Optional gem packs at $0.99, $4.99 and $9.99. Trophies and rank can't be bought.
- Target persona: Puzzle and classic-game fans, ages 12 and up
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 2–5 people
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Store listings in 20 languages. Server-timed daily world board, weekly championship with an archived hall of fame, live chat with blocking and reporting. Made by SeaCliff Apps.
