## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Get disposable US phone numbers that forward calls to your existing phone and deliver texts to a private inbox, keeping your real number private.
- Problem solved: Protects users from spam, unwanted follow-ups, and exposure of their personal phone number when sharing it with marketplaces, dating apps, vendors, and other services.
- Pricing model: 7-day free trial; Basic: $9.99/month for 5 numbers and 100 forwarded minutes; Pro: $29.99/month for 100 numbers and 1,000 forwarded minutes. Additional numbers cost $1/month on Basic or $0.50/month on Pro.
- Target persona: Individuals who want to protect their personal number when using marketplaces, dating apps, rental listings, quote services, or deliveries.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Offers real US numbers from a licensed carrier; numbers are receive-only. Available through a browser or free iPhone app.
- Markets: [Utilities](https://trustmrr.com/category/utilities), [Mobile Apps](https://trustmrr.com/category/mobile-apps), [Security](https://trustmrr.com/category/security)
