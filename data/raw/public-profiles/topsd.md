## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Open a Procreate file on any computer and get a layered PSD with every layer, mask and blend mode, without an iPad and without uploading the artwork.
- Problem solved: Procreate's PSD export only works on the iPad. Artists away from their iPad, and clients or print shops who receive a .procreate file, can't get the layers into Photoshop.
- Pricing model: Free preview and flat PNG, JPG and PDF. Layered PSD, TIFF or Krita: $3.90 per file, or $24.90 once for Unlimited. No subscription.
- Target persona: Digital artists, illustrators, Live2D and VTuber riggers, and clients or print s
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 32
- Additional info: #1 Product of the Day on Fazier (21 Oct 2026). Conversion runs in the browser with Rust compiled to WebAssembly, so artwork is never uploaded. The site also has 28 free tools for artists.
