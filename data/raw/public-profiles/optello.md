## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Provides exclusive, pay-per-lead customer enquiries sourced from its own websites and advertising, with businesses bidding for leads in their chosen industries and areas.
- Problem solved: Businesses often receive shared, resold, or poorly targeted leads. Optello routes each enquiry to one business in the relevant service area, with no subscription or lock-in.
- Pricing model: Pay per lead, with leads starting at $30. Businesses set a bid at or above Optello’s minimum price; a $50 minimum credit top-up is required. Optional monthly website leasing is also available.
- Target persona: Service businesses seeking customer enquiries in specific local areas, including air conditioning, automatic gates, demolition, and garage door businesses.
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Leads are sourced from Optello-owned websites and advertising, sold exclusively to one business, and can be disputed within 72 hours.
