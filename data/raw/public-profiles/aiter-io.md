## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: One link in, a whole campaign out. Aiter works out the customers, competitors, positioning and voice before it writes a word, so the ads sound like the business and not like a robot that skimmed the homepage.
- Problem solved: Most AI tools write before they know anything about the business, so the output comes out generic. Aiter works out the ICP, competitors, positioning and voice from a single link, then uses that context for images, copy and video in one tool.
- Pricing model: Free tier, then Begin $9.99/mo, Advance $29.99/mo and Conquer $149/mo. Yearly plans are cheaper.
- Target persona: SME owners and marketers in the US, France, Brazil, the rest of Europe and Asia
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 2–5 people
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 14,300
- Additional info: Roadmap comes with the sale: scheduled posting to socials, Analytics and Search Console results feeding the next brief, launching and managing Meta and Google ads, chat mode (built, in testing) and deep MCP for AI agents. We hand it over. Building it is up to you.
