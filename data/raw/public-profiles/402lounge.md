## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Receipts and a register for AI agents that pay with x402
- Problem solved: An x402 payment leaves only a USDC transfer to a wallet address, and an accountant cannot book that. 402lounge names the seller, writes a receipt for every payment and turns a month of them into one document per seller.
- Pricing model: Free €0 (one agent), Books €29/month, Books for teams €99/month, large companies on request. Yearly plans get 2 months free.
- Target persona: Companies whose AI agents pay with x402, and the sellers behind those APIs
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Every receipt links to its transaction on Base. MCP server listed in the official MCP registry, connects to Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. CSV and DATEV export. Dashboard in 11 languages. Made in Germany.
- Markets: [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai), [Dev Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/developer-tools), [Fintech](https://trustmrr.com/category/fintech)
