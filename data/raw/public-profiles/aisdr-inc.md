## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Generate qualified sales conversations without adding SDR headcount. Combine prospecting, research, outreach and follow-up in one platform so your team can focus on closing.
- Problem solved: B2B teams struggle to build consistent pipeline because outbound requires too many tools, manual tasks and specialist skills. Sending more messages does not solve poor targeting or irrelevant outreach.
- Pricing model: Pay as you go: $1.50 per person contacted, billed daily, with no subscription. Volume-based subscriptions, starting at $250/month and up to $5k/month.
- Target persona: B2B founders, sales and marketing teams, especially SMBs
