## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Find the ads a competitor has kept running for 30+ days and turn each one into a ready prompt for your own ad, in under a minute.
- Problem solved: Ends the hours of scrolling ad libraries and guessing which competitor ads work. An ad a brand keeps paying for month after month is a proven one, so you start from those.
- Pricing model: $79 one-time, lifetime access. The price rises $10 after every 5 sales.
- Target persona: E-commerce and DTC brand owners, performance marketers, media buyers, ad agencie
