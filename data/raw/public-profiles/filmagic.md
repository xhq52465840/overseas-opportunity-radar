## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A launch video in your brand's style, from just your URL, in minutes instead of weeks and at a fraction of agency cost.
- Problem solved: Makers and small startups need a product video to launch, but hiring a studio is expensive and editing it themselves takes days. Filmagic pulls the story, visuals and style from the website and produces the video automatically.
- Pricing model: Free tier (selected templates, lower export quality, no voiceover). Paid credit-based monthly and yearly plans unlock all templates, HD export and AI
- Target persona: Indie makers, solo founders and small startups launching products
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Team size: 1 person
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Launched October 2026. New templates shipped weekly. Includes a free device mockup tool. Voiceover available in English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese (BR).
