## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Press-and-hold voice dictation that pastes accurately formatted text into any macOS app, fully offline and private.
- Problem solved: Saves time for people who type all day, works across 10+ apps without plugins, and gives a private, offline alternative to cloud dictation tools for those with RSI or similar needs.
- Pricing model: Free (30 min/day dictation); Pro $9.99 one-time (unlimited offline dictation based on whisper models + bring your own LLM key for optional text)
- Target persona: Knowledge workers, developers, and professionals who dictate frequently
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 2–5 people
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: 100% on-device processing via a local Whisper model; no internet required.
