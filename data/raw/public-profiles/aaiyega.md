## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Create and share customizable Indian wedding invitation websites with event details, venue maps, photos, music, and RSVP—all through one editable link.
- Problem solved: Printed cards and static PDFs become outdated when plans change and make it harder to share event details, directions, and collect RSVPs from guests.
- Pricing model: Free to create and edit; one-time publishing plans: Basic $3.99, Standard $7.99, Premium $15.99. No recurring charges.
- Target persona: Indian couples and families planning weddings and sharing invitations with guests via WhatsApp.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Offers 18 invitation designs; supports multiple Indian wedding traditions and Hindi and English. Payments via UPI, cards, or netbanking.
- Markets: [Design Tools](https://trustmrr.com/category/design-tools), [SaaS](https://trustmrr.com/category/saas)
