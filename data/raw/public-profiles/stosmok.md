- Value proposition: A private, offline-friendly quit-smoking app that uses a growing dragon companion, craving support, and health recovery milestones to help users stay smoke-free.
- Problem solved: Quitting smoking is difficult, especially when cravings and triggers make it hard to maintain motivation; the app offers practical coping tools and progress reinforcement.
- Pricing model: Free to install with a 3-day free trial; one-time payment unlocks everything forever. Craving SOS tools are always free; optional dragon skins and themes are extra purchases.
