## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A chess coach you can talk to while you play. Léon explains the ideas behind each move, highlights threats on the board, and turns your mistakes into focused practice—so every game becomes a lesson.
- Problem solved: Knowing that a move is a blunder does not explain how to improve. PolyMind Chess brings explanations, questions, visual hints and targeted exercises into the same playing experience, helping learners connect engine analysis with ideas they can use in their next game.
- Pricing model: Free: 1 guest game + 10 after sign-in; unlimited analysis. Premium: US$4.99/month, unlimited games. No card to try. Cancel anytime.
- Target persona: Chess learners seeking guided practice in English or French.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: English and French. Stockfish and supported AI models run in your browser; game history stays on your device. JEV and related coaching connect to the cloud. Local models depend on device capabilities. Voice questions, visual hints and targeted exercises. Subscription payments via Stripe.
