## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Explains what's filling your Mac's disk in plain language and clears it safely, with Undo.
- Problem solved: macOS lumps hundreds of gigabytes into System Data with no explanation, so people don't know what's safe to delete when their disk fills up.
- Pricing model: Free to see everything. Diskly Pro is a one-time payment for 2 Macs with all future updates: $9 launch price until Nov 30, then $14. 7-day free trial,
- Target persona: Mac users whose disk is full, including developers with Xcode, Docker and node_m
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Team size: 1 person
- Estimated user count: 20
- Additional info: Native Apple silicon app for macOS 14+. Licence keys verify offline, no account needed.
