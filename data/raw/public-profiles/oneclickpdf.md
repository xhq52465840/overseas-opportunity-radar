## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Most PDF jobs done free in your browser with no sign-up, and the few online tools say exactly what they send and to whom.
- Problem solved: Many online PDF sites upload every file to their servers, even to merge two documents or add a signature, and cap free use unless you pay for a plan. OneClickPDF does most PDF jobs in your own browser, free and without an account, and is upfront about the few that need to go online.
- Pricing model: Most tools free. One-off $0.99 extras, no subscription: PDF to Word, premium Office to PDF, Translate (first 3 pages free), AI day pass
- Target persona: Office workers, freelancers, small businesses, students, teachers, legal and HR
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Progressive Web App that can work offline after tools are first loaded; most processing happens in the browser.
