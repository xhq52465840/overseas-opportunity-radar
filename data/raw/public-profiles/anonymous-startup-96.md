## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A lightweight Mac menu bar app that overlays realistic paper textures on the screen, making it feel more like a matte page.
- Problem solved: Bright, glossy screens can feel harsh during long periods of reading, writing, and coding; Shoji softens the screen’s appearance without changing apps or requiring hardware.
- Pricing model: Free tier; Pro is a one-time purchase, currently $3 (regularly $5), including all future updates.
- Target persona: Mac users who want a calmer, paper-like screen for reading, writing, or coding.
- Audience type: [B2C](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2C)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Independently made by one developer; native Swift app for macOS 13 or later, supports up to 3 Macs per Pro license, and requires no account.
