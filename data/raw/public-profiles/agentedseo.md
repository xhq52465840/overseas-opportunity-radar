## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Lets your AI agent do WordPress SEO safely: every change is previewed, approved by you and undoable.
- Problem solved: Cuts the manual work of meta, schema, redirects and alt text, without handing an AI agent an admin password.
- Pricing model: Early Adopter lifetime licences until 1 Dec: $49 Solo (1 site), $129 Studio (25 sites), $199 Agency (unlimited). From 2 Dec: $49, $99 and $149 a year.
- Target persona: WordPress site owners, SEOs and agencies who use Claude, Cursor or Codex
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Built-in MCP server plus REST API and WP-CLI. 46 features, imports from Yoast, Rank Math and 8 other plugins, 15-day refund. Tested on 10 WordPress sites over 90 days.
