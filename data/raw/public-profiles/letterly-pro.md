## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Automatically turns each newsletter edition into a week of social posts in the writer’s voice, then schedules and publishes them across X, LinkedIn, Threads, and Facebook.
- Problem solved: Newsletter writers often lack time to promote each edition on social media, leading to inconsistent posting and missed opportunities to grow their audience.
- Pricing model: $29/month with a 7-day free trial; one plan with all features.
- Target persona: Newsletter writers and creators who want to promote their editions on social media.
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: Works with newsletters that provide an RSS feed, including beehiiv, Substack, Ghost, Buttondown, and Mailchimp. Founder reports growing his newsletter by 546 subscribers in three months using Quibr.
