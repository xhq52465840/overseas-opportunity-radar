## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Turn everyday phones into fleet gps trackers. View your devices on a map with real-time locations and alerts.
- Problem solved: No GPS devices to buy, install, or maintain. Instant visibility of devices on a live map with real-time status, Geo Zones, and alerts.
- Pricing model: $30 TTD/month per device or $1 TTD/day per device
- Target persona: Fleet Owners, Drivers, Taxi & Transport Networks, Security & Guarding, Schools & Student Transport
