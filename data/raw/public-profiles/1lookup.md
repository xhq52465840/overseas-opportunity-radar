## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: A single API and universal credit balance for validating, enriching, and analyzing phone, email, IP, company, social, and web data in real time.
- Problem solved: Businesses struggle with inaccurate or fragmented contact and business data, which leads to bad leads, fraud, poor data quality, and wasted outreach. 1Lookup consolidates fast verification and enrichment tools into one service.
- Pricing model: Pay-as-you-go using universal credits; 1 credit per phone, email, or IP check. 7-day free trial with credits; no setup fees or minimums.
- Target persona: Businesses and developers integrating data validation, fraud prevention, contact enrichment, and prospecting into CRMs, apps, and workflows, especially SMBs and growing businesses.
- Audience type: [B2B](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=B2B)
- Estimated user count: 0
- Additional info: 76 data products behind one API; supports REST API, MCP, and integrations including HubSpot and Salesforce. Millions of lookups processed.
