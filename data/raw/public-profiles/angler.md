## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: The laptop lid becomes a 3D camera move instead of a black screen.
- Problem solved: Closing a MacBook is abrupt. Angler gives the desktop depth and motion as the lid goes down.
- Pricing model: $5.99 one time, 24 hour free trial, up to 3 Macs
- Target persona: MacBook owners who care how their desktop looks
- Estimated user count: 87
- Additional info: 11,380 lines of Swift. Notarized universal app. Needs macOS 13+ and a MacBook Pro or Air from 2019 or later, because it reads the lid sensor.
